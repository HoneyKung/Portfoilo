Digital Puzzle Box

A physical board that works as the controller for a puzzle box living on screen — pick a face, solve its puzzle digitally, and find the real key to finish.

In short: A physical board that controls a puzzle box on screen. I led the team and built the board, the maze screen and the Arduino code (I wrote several parts myself; AI wrote others) — course project, done.

When
Year 2, Term 2 (2025)
Team / role
Team lead, team of 3
Tools
Arduino · ESP32 · Processing · breadboard · servo · potentiometer · ultrasonic & IR sensors · LEDs
Status
done

Problem

A normal puzzle box is solved physically, in your hands. I wanted a digital twin: you hold the real object like a console, and the puzzles live on screen — puzzles that only work digitally — so touching the real thing changes the digital world.

What I decided

  • Planned first: the real box as the controller. Turn to a face of the box, and the digital box on screen turns to the same face.
  • Change the plan when the sensor fails. The gyro (MPU) didn’t work at all, so I switched from a box you flip to a flat board where you choose which face to play.
  • Six puzzles, one per face — Color Match, Simon Says, Maze, Distance Puzzle, Keyhole and Clock Time.
  • The last key is real. The Keyhole screen only hints where the key is hidden on the physical board; you find it and plug it in. Clear everything and the treasure box beside the board opens, with candy inside.

Result

Built on a breadboard with one combined win condition across all six games.
