Original fantasy novel series — writing & world design

An original fantasy novel series in Thai that I write myself, set in a world I designed myself — its races, a power system with several layers, its history, and a main cast of more than 10 characters.

In short: My own fantasy series, written in Thai. I write the story and design the world. Draft so far: volume 1 complete, volume 2 unfinished (~50 chapters in all). Now rewriting the series, starting from its lore and character sheets.

Problem

A long story with a large cast only works if its world stays consistent from one chapter to the next: what each race and power can and cannot do, what has already happened in the world’s history, and how each character would really act.

What I decided

  • Design the world, not just the plot. I designed the races of the world, a power system with several layers, and the world’s history, so events in the story follow from the world’s own rules.
  • Reference documents for continuity. I keep lore (the world’s history and rules) and character reference documents, and check the chapters against them so details stay consistent from chapter to chapter.
  • Rewrite the drafts. With volume 1 complete and volume 2 unfinished in draft, I am now rewriting the whole series, starting from its lore and character sheets.
  • One cast across my work. The same characters appear in my character sheets, my animation and OC Chat.

Result

Draft so far: volume 1 complete, volume 2 unfinished (~50 chapters in all). Now rewriting the series, starting from its lore and character sheets. The story itself is not shown here.

My role vs AI

Me: all of it — I write the story and design the world myself. I write all of the prose myself; AI is not used to write the story.
