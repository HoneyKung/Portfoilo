Original fantasy novel series: writing & world design

I write an original fantasy novel series in Thai and design its world, from its races and layered power system to its history. With more than 10 main characters, I use lore and character reference documents to check continuity across chapters.

In short: Solo. Volume 1 is drafted in full and volume 2 is unfinished (~50 chapters in all). Now rewriting the series, starting from its lore and character sheets.

Problem

Across about 50 chapters and 10+ main characters, I need to keep track of what each race and power can and cannot do, what has already happened in the world’s history, and how each character would really act.

What I decided

  • World rules first. I designed the races of the world, a power system with several layers, and the world’s history, so events in the story follow from the world’s own rules.
  • Reference documents for continuity. I keep lore (the world’s history and rules) and character reference documents, and check the chapters against them so details stay consistent from chapter to chapter.
  • One cast across my work. The same characters appear in my character sheets, my animation and OC Chat.

Result

The series is being rewritten, starting with lore and character reference documents. The story itself is not shown here.

My role

All mine: the writing and the world design. I write every line of the story and design the world myself.
