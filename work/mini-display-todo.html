Mini Display To-Do List

A concept for a small desk device that shows only the task you should do next, so you don’t have to pick up your phone to check your list.

In short: A course mini-project for a team of 3. As team lead I did the concept, diagrams, concept renders and choice of components. It is a concept only.

When
Year 3, Term 1 (2025)
Team / role
Team lead, team of 3 (course mini-project)
Planned hardware and software
ESP32 · OLED · LED · Firebase Realtime Database · web app
Status
concept

Problem

Working at a computer, you pick up your phone to check what’s next on your to-do list, and end up scrolling feeds or reading other notifications. IoT notice displays available today mostly just show information one way. You can’t update anything from them.

The idea

  • Focus on one task. The OLED screen shows only the first task to do.
  • Two-way. Press the round Done button and the task is marked done in the cloud and the next one appears. The triangle Progress button shows how much of today’s list is done, as a percentage.
  • A light for overdue tasks. An LED stays on once a task is past its deadline.
  • Retro handheld shape. The shape is inspired by retro handheld game consoles, so it sits on a desk like a small game device.
  • How it connects. Tasks are created and edited in a web app, stored in Firebase Realtime Database, and pulled over Wi-Fi by an ESP32. Deadlines are checked against internet time (NTP).

Result

A concept, presented in the course. Planned next steps were battery power and links to apps such as Google Tasks or Trello.

My role

I was team lead, designed the concept, made the diagrams and concept renders, and chose the components. My teammates handled the report and research.
