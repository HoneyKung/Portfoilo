Stardew Valley mod

An NPC who feels alive: Raphael keeps his own daily routine, can follow you or lead you somewhere, and talks to fit the situation.

Year
Year 4, Term 1
Team / role
Designer and director (character, house and yard layout, behaviour rules, final say on script); an AI team writes the code and script
Tools
Stardew Valley 1.6 · SMAPI · C# · Content Patcher · pixel art
Status
in progress

Problem

Make one NPC feel truly alive — not a character who stands in place reading lines, but one who has his own day, can do things with the player on request, and speaks to fit the moment. And build it so the same systems can be reused for other characters later.

What I decided

  • Two layers. Eternity Core, a reusable C# framework that holds the behaviour systems, plus Content Patcher packs that hold the character’s data — so another character can be added without rewriting the engine.
  • The character is mine. I designed Raphael and his whole personality, and chose the designs and visuals.
  • His home, planned first. I designed the layout of Raphael’s house and front yard myself. The house and yard I designed are still at the design stage; the game currently uses a placeholder house.
  • Clear behaviour rules. The NPC does one thing at a time; a new command replaces the old one.
  • Final say on the script. I set the script rules and have the final say: I overrule cuts and restore lines I want kept.

Result

Working in the game now:

  • a daily routine across maps — Raphael walks his own schedule through town;
  • routines that read the game state (his museum stops follow what the player has donated);
  • a custom talk-topic menu to start activities with him;
  • lead mode — he guides the player to a destination with lines that fit the moment;
  • follow mode (still being tuned);
  • custom items (Eternity Goods);
  • extra Thai dialogue for townsfolk, depending on weather, time and place;
  • an in-game automated test suite with 200+ cases.

Still in progress.
