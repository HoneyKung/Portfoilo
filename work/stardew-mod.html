Stardew Valley mod

An NPC (a non-player character) who feels alive: Raphael keeps his own daily routine, can follow you, lead you somewhere or come to visit, and talks to fit the situation.

In short: A Stardew Valley mod with Raphael, an NPC I designed who keeps his own daily routine. I designed him, his rules and his walking sprite; AI tools write the code and draft the script — in progress.

When
Year 4, Term 1 (2026)
Team / role
Designer and director (character, house and yard layout, behaviour rules, final say on script); AI tools write the code and draft the script
Tools
Stardew Valley 1.6 · SMAPI and Content Patcher (the standard Stardew modding tools) · C# · pixel art · a Thai word-wrap mod (C#)
Status
in progress

Problem

Make one NPC feel truly alive — not a character who stands in place reading lines, but one who has his own day, can do things with the player on request, and speaks to fit the situation. And build it so the same systems can be reused for other characters later.

What I decided

  • Two layers. Eternity Core, a reusable C# framework that holds the behaviour systems, plus Content Patcher packs that hold the character’s data — so another character can be added without rewriting the engine.
  • The character is mine. I designed Raphael and his whole personality, and chose the designs and visuals.
  • His home, planned first. I designed the layout of Raphael’s house and front yard myself. The house and yard I designed are still at the design stage; the game currently uses a placeholder house.
  • Clear behaviour rules. The NPC does one thing at a time; a new command replaces the old one.
  • A packed lunch on days working away. On days he works away from home, Raphael brings a packed lunch.
  • Planned: a party system. Companions will surround the player instead of walking in a line, so no one looks left behind.
  • Fix Thai line breaks in code. Thai is written without spaces between words, so I added a Thai word-wrap mod (C#) that breaks dialogue lines between words.
  • Final say on the script. I set the script rules and have the final say: when AI cuts lines, I overrule it and restore the ones I want kept.

Result

Working in the game now:

  • a daily routine across maps — Raphael walks his own schedule through town;
  • routines that read the game state (his museum stops follow what the player has donated);
  • a custom talk-topic menu to start activities with him;
  • lead mode — he guides the player to a destination with lines that fit the situation;
  • follow mode (still being tuned);
  • visit mode — he comes to visit the player;
  • custom items (Eternity Goods, the mod’s pack of new items);
  • extra Thai dialogue for townsfolk, depending on weather, season, day and place, with Thai line breaks handled by the word-wrap mod;
  • 224 automated in-game checks (latest run: 192 pass, 6 fail, 26 skipped); 3 of the 6 failures are follow-mode cases still being tuned.

Still in progress.
