Mini paint palette

A small palette with many small, deep wells, for painting small pieces that only need a little paint.

In short: A small 18-well paint palette I designed in Tinkercad and printed, for painting small pieces — in use.

When
Year 3, Term 2 (2026)
Team / role
Solo · self-initiated
Tools
Tinkercad · 3D printing (PLA)
Status
in use

Problem

Palettes with small wells usually have only 5–10 of them; I couldn’t find one with many small wells for painting small pieces, which use very little paint.

What I decided

  • Small wells to help control how much paint I use.
  • Many wells in a small tray — 18 small wells in a 109 × 75 mm tray.
  • Deep wells, so the paint is less exposed to air.
  • A channel along the side as space to test, swatch or mix paint; the round notches on its rim are brush rests.

Result

Printed and in use.
