OC Companion

A small device that is one customer’s original character (OC): a small AMOLED screen is the whole face, with figure-style printed hair.

Year
The idea came together with the OC Chat web app
Team / role
Solo · self-initiated
Tools
Small AMOLED display · 3D-printed hair (planned) · on-computer simulator
Status
in design

Problem

Give one customer’s character a small physical body: one device per character, designed to sit on a desk and to be made as a keychain.

What I decided

  • The whole face is the screen — a small AMOLED display, so the character’s face lives on it.
  • Hair printed like a figure around the screen face.
  • One device = one customer’s OC.
  • Desk and keychain — designed to sit on a desk and to be made as a keychain.
  • Touch on the face + motion sensor (poke, pat, shake, tilt) instead of a touch head.
  • Always alive: blinks and breathes, timed like hand-drawn animation (on twos) rather than smooth rig motion.

Result

Still in design, currently a simulation on the computer. No hardware built yet. First simulator round works: it loads my layered drawing (PSD) and plays a blink on the device-sized screen.

Sketches & process

My role vs AI

Me: concept, product decisions, character art in layers, and (next) rigging the face myself. AI: wrote the specs and simulator code from my decisions; a separate AI reviewer checked them.
