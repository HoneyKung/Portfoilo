OC Companion

A palm-sized device (about 4–5 cm per side), made to order as one buyer’s own original character (OC): a small AMOLED screen is the whole face, with figure-style sculpted hair. It sits on a desk, travels with you, and will be made as a keychain.

In short: A concept for a palm-sized device, made to order for one buyer’s own character (OC), whose whole face is a screen. I did the concept and the character art; AI wrote the simulator — in design, no hardware yet.

When
2026 · the idea came together with OC Chat
Team / role
Solo · self-initiated
Tools
Small AMOLED display · sculpted hair (planned) · on-computer simulator
Status
in design

Problem

Give a buyer’s own character (OC) a small physical body, made to order: one device per character — a palm-sized device that sits on a desk or travels with you.

What I decided

  • The whole face is the screen — a small AMOLED display, so the character’s face lives on it.
  • Figure-style sculpted hair around the screen face.
  • Made to order: one device per buyer’s own character (OC).
  • Desk, pocket and keychain — palm-sized, so one body works on a desk, in a pocket and on a keychain.
  • Touch on the face + motion sensor (poke, pat, shake, tilt): the touchscreen face and a motion sensor take the place of a touch sensor on the head.
  • Always alive: blinks and breathes, timed like hand-drawn animation — mostly on twos — a new drawing every 2 frames (blink on ones, breathing on fours) — rather than smooth rig motion.

Result

Still in design, currently a simulation on the computer. No hardware built yet. First simulator round works: it reads a layered PSD in my template and plays a test blink on the device-sized screen; my own face drawing goes in next.

My role vs AI

Me: concept, product decisions, character art in layers, and (next) rigging the face myself. AI: researched options and wrote the technical specs for me to decide on, and wrote the simulator code; a separate AI reviewer checked them.
