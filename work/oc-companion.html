OC Companion

A palm-sized device (about 4–5 cm per side), made to order as one buyer’s own original character (OC): a small AMOLED screen is the whole face, with figure-style sculpted hair. Designed for a desk, travel and use as a keychain.

In short: Solo and self-initiated. I did the concept and the character art, and AI wrote the simulator. It is still in design, with no hardware yet.

When
2026 · the idea came together with OC Chat
Team / role
Solo · self-initiated
Tools
Small AMOLED display · sculpted hair (planned) · on-computer simulator
Status
in design

Problem

Give a buyer’s own character (OC) a small physical body, made to order. One palm-sized device per character, which sits on a desk or travels with you.

What I decided

  • The whole face is the screen. It is a small AMOLED display, so the character’s face lives on it.
  • Figure-style sculpted hair around the screen face.
  • Made to order: one device per buyer’s own character (OC).
  • Desk, pocket and keychain. It is palm-sized, so one body works on a desk, in a pocket and on a keychain.
  • Touch on the face + motion sensor (poke, pat, shake, tilt): the touchscreen face and a motion sensor take the place of a touch sensor on the head.
  • Planned idle animation: it blinks and breathes, timed like hand-drawn animation rather than smooth rig motion. It is mostly on twos, a new drawing every 2 frames, with the blink on ones and breathing on fours.

Result

Still in design, currently a simulation on the computer. No hardware built yet. First simulator round works: it reads a layered PSD in my template and plays a test blink on the device-sized screen. Next, I will add the character face artwork I drew.

My role vs AI

I came up with the concept, made the product decisions and drew the character art in layers. Next, I will rig the face myself. AI researched options, wrote technical specs for me to decide on and wrote the simulator code, with a separate AI reviewer checking that work.
